Deleting your data

VegDrop · Last updated 17 August 2026

You can ask us to delete your VegDrop account and the personal data attached to it. This page explains how to ask, what gets removed, and what we are required to keep.

How to request deletion

Email devgooty@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number your account uses, with the subject line Delete my account.

We will:

  1. Confirm the request is genuinely from you, so nobody can delete someone else's account.
  2. Acknowledge your request within 24 hours.
  3. Complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm when it is done.
We ask you to email rather than offering a one-tap button because deletion is irreversible and your account is tied to a phone number that signs you in. Confirming the request comes from you is the point, not an obstacle.

What is removed

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your saved delivery addresses and locations.
  • Your account's ability to sign in. All sessions on every device are ended immediately.
  • For shopkeepers: your stored bank details, including the encrypted account number.

Once your phone number and email are released, they are free to use again if you ever want to sign up afresh.

What we keep, and why

KeptReason
Completed order records Tax and accounting law requires us to retain records of sales, and the shop you bought from needs its own record of what it sold.
Payment and wallet ledger entries These are an append-only financial record. Removing entries would make balances impossible to reconcile and would break records the payment provider also holds.

These retained records are detached from your identity where we can do so, and are not used to contact you or to rebuild a profile of you.

If you only want some data removed

You do not have to delete everything. Email the same address to correct your details, remove a saved delivery address, or ask what data we hold about you.

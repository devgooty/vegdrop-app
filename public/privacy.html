Privacy Policy

VegDrop · Last updated 17 August 2026

VegDrop is a hyperlocal grocery delivery service. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It covers the VegDrop customer app and the shopkeeper and delivery partner apps.

Who we are

VegDrop is operated by S Revanth Gopal, as a sole proprietor.
Contact: devgooty@gmail.com

What we collect

DataWhy
Phone number This is how you sign in. VegDrop has no passwords — proving control of your number is what authenticates you.
Email address (optional) Receipts, and a copy of your sign-in code if you have verified the address.
Name Shown to the shopkeeper and delivery partner handling your order.
Delivery addresses and location To find nearby shops and to deliver to you. Location is used when you pick a delivery point on the map.
Order history To show you past orders, handle refunds, and settle payments with shops.
Wallet transactions To maintain your wallet balance as an auditable record.
Delivery partner location (partners only) Shared with the shopkeeper and customer while a delivery is in progress, so they can see where the order is.
Bank details (shopkeepers only) Legal name, bank name, account number, IFSC and UPI ID, so we can pay you. Required before you can list products.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or bank password — those go directly to Razorpay. We keep only a payment reference and the amount, so we can match a payment to an order.

WhatsApp and SMS

We use WhatsApp and SMS to send you a sign-in code, and to let you verify your number by sending us a code from your own phone. When you message us, we receive your phone number and the text of that message, and use it only to complete that verification. We do not send marketing messages, and we do not reply to messages you send to our verification number.

Your sign-in code is a credential. Anyone who can read it can sign in as you. We will never ask you for it — not by phone, not by message, not by email.

How we protect it

  • Bank account numbers are encrypted at rest and only ever shown to you masked to the last four digits.
  • Sign-in codes are stored only as a one-way hash, never as readable text.
  • Your session token is held in memory in your browser, not in browser storage, so a malicious script on the page cannot read it.
  • Shopkeepers see only what they need to fulfil your order. They do not get your contact details or your order history with other shops.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Who else sees your data

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • The shop and delivery partner handling your order — the minimum needed to prepare and deliver it.
  • Razorpay — to take payments and pay shopkeepers.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — to deliver and receive verification messages.
  • Our hosting and database providers — who store the data on our behalf.
  • Authorities — where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is open, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Verification codes and challenges expire within minutes and are deleted automatically.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and its data. Some records — completed orders and payments — must be kept for legal and accounting reasons even after an account is closed. To make a request, email devgooty@gmail.com.

Children

VegDrop is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
